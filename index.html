About Us

A Tanzanian supermarket built on trust

Mkwabi Supermarket was founded on 11 March 2017 in Tanga with a simple promise: good-quality products at fair prices. What began as a single store has grown into a network of four branches across Tanzania — Tanga, and Muhimbili, Kigamboni and SGR Station in Dar es Salaam.

Every branch stocks the same range: fresh bakery and butchery, beverages, snacks, pantry staples, dairy, fresh produce, kitchen utensils, household essentials, personal care and stationery. Wherever you shop with us, you find the same products and the same standard.

We are an in-store experience: you browse and plan here, then visit a branch to choose your items yourself. We do not deliver — our teams are there to help you face to face.
